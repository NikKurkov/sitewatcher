# Compact overview header

Approved in conversation on 2026-11-04 after UX and graphic design review.

Move the owner overview's informational monitoring summary immediately after the
SiteWatcher logo. Keep its heading and domain counts in two compact lines without
a separate card. Preserve the existing status meanings, translations and controls.
Move the timestamp and history periods into a narrow row above the table.

Approaches: moving the entire card into the header would overload the available
width; leaving the card in place would not free table space. The approved approach
moves the status into the header and keeps history controls beside the results.

Use existing fonts and semantic palette variables, including dark mode. On narrow
screens allow the summary to wrap below the logo without truncation. Keep all
actions accessible and touch targets at least 44px. Only change the owner overview;
public, domain-detail and settings pages retain their existing header structure.
No deployment is included.
